How to Play:

Unscramble the letters to form a word! You have 60 seconds to solve as many words as possible.

  • Correct answer: +10 points (plus streak bonus)
  • Streak: +2 bonus points per consecutive correct answer
  • Press Enter or click Submit to check your answer
  • Click Skip Word if you get stuck
